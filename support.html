BioCue Support

Quick answers for getting the most out of your circadian schedule, alarms, widgets, and subscriptions.

My morning alarm didn't trigger—what should I check?

Make sure Notifications and AlarmKit permissions are allowed in iOS Settings → Apps → BioCue. When using the Red-Shift Nightstand Clock overnight, keep your iPhone plugged in and ensure Focus/Silent modes allow Alarms.

How do I sync Apple Health sleep data?

Open Settings → Apple Health Sync inside BioCue (or iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → BioCue) and turn on Sleep read access and Time in Daylight write access. BioCue automatically backfills your past 7 nights on-device.

Why isn't my Widget or Live Activity updating?

Widgets refresh automatically on cue completions and wake-time shifts. Make sure Live Activities are enabled under iOS Settings → Apps → BioCue → Live Activities. If a widget looks stale after a fresh install, open BioCue once to sync the App Group snapshot.

How do I restore my BioCue Pro purchase or cancel a subscription?

To restore a prior purchase on a new device, open BioCue → Settings → Privacy & Legal → Restore StoreKit Purchases. To manage or cancel an active subscription, open iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → BioCue.
